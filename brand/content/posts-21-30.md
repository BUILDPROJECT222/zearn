## 23 — Every buy is a lot
Image: `23.png`
```
Buying more $ZEARN never resets your clock.

Every buy opens a new lot with its own unlock timer. Your old lots keep unlocking.

```

## 24 — Sells cut the newest lot first
Image: `24.png`
```
Selling part of your bag? $ZEARN cuts your newest lot first.

Your oldest, most-unlocked lots survive.
Unlocked ZEC you haven't claimed stays yours.
Only the still-locked part of a sold lot goes back to the pool.

Old hands stay rewarded.
```

## 25 — Don't shuffle your bag
Image: `25.png`
```
Pro tip: don't shuffle your $ZEARN between your own wallets.

To the Hold Pool it looks like a sell and a buy, so the unlock clock restarts at 0%.

Claim what's unlocked first. Then move, if you really have to.
```
